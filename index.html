Entry 02 — About

About

I started writing code by taking apart small automation scripts for chores I didn't want to do by hand — that curiosity turned into a Computer Science degree and a habit of finishing side projects I probably should have scoped down. Right now I split my time between coursework in distributed systems and machine learning, and shipping small full‑stack projects end to end: schema, API, interface, deploy.

I care about software that fails loudly in development and quietly in production — clear errors, sane defaults, and interfaces that don't need a manual. Outside of class, I'm usually reading changelogs I have no immediate use for, or helping juniors in my campus dev community debug their first Git merge conflict.
